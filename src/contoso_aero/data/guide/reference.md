# reference.py

Holds the fictional operators, plants, modules, suppliers and shops.

* Uses Microsoft's standard fictional brands so no real company is implied.
* Defines four airlines whose 50 aircraft give exactly 100 engine positions.
* Defines two plants with two assembly lines each.
* Lists the six engine modules with their tracked part, part number, supplier and stations.
* Fixes the flight hours per cycle used to turn cycles into hours.
