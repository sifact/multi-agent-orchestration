# SB-CX7-72-0051: HPC rotor stack bolt preload verification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document | SB-CX7-72-0051 |
| Type | Service bulletin |
| Effective | 2026-08-14 |
| Applies to | Contoso Aero Engines CX-7 |

## Reason

Shop visits found low rotor stack bolt preload on HPC modules built at station HPC-04
(Riverside Assembly, line L2). Torque wrench TW-118 was under-reading
(see NCR-2025-0388). Low preload lets the blisks move and rub, which speeds HPC deterioration.

## Affected engines

CX-7 engines whose HPC module was built at HPC-04 between 2025-06-02 and
2025-07-18.

## Required action

1. Borescope HPC stages 3 to 5 every 50 cycles.
2. Trend P30 and T50 at every report against CX7-EHM-002 limits.
3. At the next shop visit, re-torque the rotor stack to specification.
